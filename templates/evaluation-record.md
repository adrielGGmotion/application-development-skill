## Evidence

Link the relevant outputs, diffs, commands, and observations. Record the actual result of each check. Use sanitized fixtures and avoid personal data or credentials.

## Limits

Record unavailable checks, untested platforms, confounding differences, and cases used while writing the module. Identify held-out cases separately.

## Decision

State what the evidence supports, what needs revision, and whether the module's status should change. One successful example does not establish general effectiveness.
