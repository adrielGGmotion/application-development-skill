# Module proposal

Copy this template for a new proposal. Replace the prompts with the relevant details and remove sections that do not apply.

## Scope

Module name, related inventory IDs, and proposed status.

## Problem

Describe a concrete omission. What triggers it, who is affected, and what goes wrong?

## Applicability

State when the module should activate, which platforms or features it covers, and when it should stay out of the way.

## Proposed behavior

List the rules and their observable outcomes. Distinguish shared behavior from platform-specific details.

## Boundaries and overlap

Identify existing rules to reuse, neighboring scopes, and anything deliberately excluded.

## Sources

Record primary-source URLs, applicable versions, and dates checked. Label project recommendations and unresolved assumptions separately.

## Evaluation

Describe representative tasks, likely failure cases, and at least one non-applicable case. State what evidence would support or reject the proposed guidance.

## Cost and tradeoffs

Explain the expected reading/tool cost and any extra work the module might cause. Identify what belongs in an optional reference instead of the usual reading path.
